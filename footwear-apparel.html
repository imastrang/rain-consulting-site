What We Do

Footwear & Apparel

Deep expertise in product creation, calendar management, and go-to-market for footwear and apparel brands from concept through delivery.

Our Approach

Industry knowledge. Operator discipline.

We bring genuine footwear and apparel expertise to every engagement. This is not generalist consulting mapped onto your industry. We know the product creation calendar, the factory relationships, the material lead times, and the retail sell-in dynamics. That knowledge accelerates everything.

Product creation management

End-to-end oversight of the create-to-deliver process, on time and on spec.

Calendar management

Keep complex seasonal calendars moving across design, development, and commercialization.

Supplier & factory coordination

Relationship management, quality oversight, and accountability across your partner base.

Delivery

From concept to shelf, on time, every time.

We've managed footwear and apparel programs for global brands with complex multi-country supply chains and demanding retail timelines. We know how to keep product moving, how to escalate when things shift, and how to land launches that meet the market.

Global sourcing support

Navigate factory selection, development, and compliance across regions.

Go-to-market coordination

Sales, retail, and marketing alignment for launches that land clean.

Line planning & merchandising support

Assortment architecture, buy depth, and sell-through strategy.
