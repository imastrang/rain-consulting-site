Cost Teardown Process Reengineering and Cross-Functional Alignment

Global footwear brand · Project Manager · 4 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientGlobal footwear brand
RoleProject Manager
Duration4 months
CategoryStrategy & Operations

The Challenge

A global footwear brand's cost teardown sessions — where design, development, and costing teams reviewed and optimized product costs — had become inefficient. Meetings lacked standardized tools, documentation was inconsistent, and decisions weren't being captured and followed through reliably. The process was consuming time without generating the cross-functional alignment it was designed to produce.

What We Did

  • Reengineered the cost teardown process in close collaboration with design and development teams, identifying what was working and what needed to change.
  • Developed comprehensive documentation resources, standardized templates, and reporting tools to bring structure and consistency to the teardown process.
  • Created new meeting workflows that clearly defined roles, pre-work requirements, and decision documentation standards for each session.
  • Improved cross-functional collaboration by aligning design, development, and costing teams on a shared process with clear expectations and follow-through mechanisms.

The Results

The reengineered cost teardown process significantly improved meeting efficiency and reduced time wasted on unproductive sessions. Standardized tools and documentation gave teams a consistent foundation, and cross-functional collaboration improved as all three teams gained shared visibility into cost decisions. The new process was adopted across the organization.
