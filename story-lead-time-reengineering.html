Lead Time Reengineering: Aligning Product Launch Across Functions

Global footwear brand · Project Manager · 7 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientGlobal footwear brand
RoleProject Manager
Duration7 months
CategorySupply Chain

The Challenge

A global footwear brand's First Delivery Date (FDD) process had grown inconsistent, leading to misaligned expectations between product development, marketing, supply/demand planning, and logistics. Product launches were missing their intended market windows, and teams were working from different timing assumptions. The organization needed a process reengineering effort that would create consistent, reliable lead time management across all functions.

What We Did

  • Analyzed existing lead time processes to identify inefficiencies, conflicting assumptions, and gaps in cross-functional coordination.
  • Collaborated with product development, marketing, supply/demand planning, and logistics to redesign the FDD process with shared inputs and clear accountabilities.
  • Implemented revised lead time strategies and FDD frameworks that aligned cross-functional timing expectations and created clearer market release milestones.
  • Trained teams on the new FDD process, ensuring consistent application and adoption across all relevant departments.

The Results

The reengineered FDD process produced more consistent and market-aligned product launches. Cross-functional teams gained a shared framework for lead time planning, reducing the back-and-forth that had previously caused delays and misalignment. The training program ensured the new process stuck beyond the engagement.
