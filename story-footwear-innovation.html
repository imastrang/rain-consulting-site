Footwear Innovation: Materials R&D and Factory Partnerships

Major athletic brand · Project Manager · 19 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientMajor athletic brand
RoleProject Manager
Duration19 months
CategoryFootwear & Apparel

The Challenge

A major athletic brand's innovation team was pushing the boundaries of footwear materials and manufacturing — but translating early-stage R&D into production-ready innovations required managing complex external partnerships, rapid prototyping cycles, and the operational bridge between the innovation center and factory floors in Asia.

What We Did

  • Collaborated with Huntsman Polyurethane and Desma Equipment to co-develop a proprietary foam material and a new manufacturing process tailored to the brand's performance requirements.
  • Managed rapid prototyping, material testing, and finished goods testing cycles to validate product quality and performance at each stage.
  • Oversaw sampling and prototyping in the innovation center, managing the design-to-manufacturing pipeline for running and basketball products.
  • Traveled to Vietnam and Taiwan to research best-in-class manufacturing methods and build partnerships with factory innovation centers.

The Results

The partnership produced a proprietary foam material and manufacturing process that met the brand's performance standards for running and basketball products. Prototyping cycles were managed efficiently, accelerating the path from concept to production-ready samples. Factory visits in Asia built durable innovation partnerships that supported ongoing product development.
