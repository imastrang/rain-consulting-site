Supply Chain

Six-Workstream Supply Chain Optimization for a Global Sportswear Brand

Global sportswear brand · Project Manager · 13 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientGlobal sportswear brand
RoleProject Manager
Duration13 months
CategorySupply Chain

The Challenge

A global sportswear brand's North American supply planning organization was carrying excess inventory, experiencing inconsistent on-time availability, and relying on reporting tools with poor data integrity. The fragmented nature of supply planning processes created inefficiencies across multiple teams, and leadership needed a structured, multi-workstream program to address root causes — not just symptoms.

What We Did

  • Managed a program of six concurrent workstreams targeting excess inventory reduction, on-time availability improvement, and process efficiency.
  • Conducted extensive current-state documentation and root cause analysis across supply planning functions to identify where processes were breaking down.
  • Led reporting upgrades from legacy systems to MicroStrategy, significantly improving data integrity and giving planners access to actionable insights.
  • Facilitated change management for approximately 50 team members, managing communication, process retraining, and the people side of the transition.

The Results

The program delivered measurable improvements in supply chain efficiency across all six workstreams. Excess inventory levels declined, on-time availability improved, and the team gained a credible, data-driven reporting foundation in MicroStrategy. Change management support helped 50 team members adopt new processes without significant disruption to ongoing operations.
