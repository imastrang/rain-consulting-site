Carrier Rate Renegotiation for Small Wholesale Shipments

Global footwear brand · Project Manager · 4 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientGlobal footwear brand
RoleProject Manager
Duration4 months
CategoryStrategy & Operations

The Challenge

A global footwear brand was absorbing high per-shipment costs on small wholesale orders because carrier rates hadn't been renegotiated to reflect current volume levels and market conditions. The burden was falling disproportionately on shipments to smaller wholesale accounts, straining account economics and creating friction in sales operations relationships.

What We Did

  • Led a structured project to renegotiate UPS and FedEx rates specifically on shipments to small wholesale customers, preparing the business case and leading carrier negotiations.
  • Analyzed shipping data in detail to quantify the cost impact on small wholesale shipments and identify the highest-value negotiation levers.
  • Successfully negotiated new rate structures with both carriers, reducing per-shipment costs for targeted account segments.
  • Implemented new shipping strategies and guidelines to optimize logistics and reduce ongoing shipping expenses.

The Results

New rates were negotiated with both UPS and FedEx, reducing per-shipment costs for small wholesale accounts. The outcome improved account economics and strengthened sales operations relationships with strategic accounts, removing a friction point that had been absorbing management attention for months.
