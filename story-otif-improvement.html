Supply Chain

OTIF Transformation: 0–20% to 70–80% in Eight Months

Global footwear brand · Project Manager · 8 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientGlobal footwear brand
RoleProject Manager
Duration8 months
CategorySupply Chain

The Challenge

A global footwear brand was consistently missing on-time and in-full delivery commitments to wholesale customers — with OTIF rates averaging just 0–20% monthly. The causes were fragmented across sourcing, supply planning, logistics, distribution, and customer service. No single team owned the problem, and leadership needed a structured program to drive accountability across all functions simultaneously.

What We Did

  • Directed a cross-functional global operational efficiency program, establishing clear ownership and workstreams across sourcing, supply planning, logistics, distribution, and customer service.
  • Provided operational support to functional owners in each area, helping teams identify root causes, set targets, and take accountable action on delivery improvement.
  • Tracked plans, workstreams, and goals in a single source of truth, assessing progress against milestones and reporting to leadership weekly.
  • Maintained consistent focus on the OTIF metric throughout the eight-month program, ensuring all workstreams stayed connected to the customer delivery outcome.

The Results

OTIF delivery rates to wholesale customers increased from 0–20% to 70–80% monthly over eight months — a transformative improvement driven by cross-functional alignment, clear ownership, and consistent tracking. The program established durable processes across all five functional areas that the organization continued to build on after the engagement ended.

OTIF to wholesale customers improved from 0–20% to 70–80% monthly in eight months. One of the most measurable supply chain turnarounds in the firm’s history.
