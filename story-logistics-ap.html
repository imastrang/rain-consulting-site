Logistics & AP Software Integration for a Global Malt Producer

Global malt producer · Consultant · 3 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientGlobal malt producer
RoleConsultant
Duration3 months
CategoryTechnology

The Challenge

A global malt producer's accounts payable team was operating on disconnected processes, manually reconciling logistics data against payable transactions. The lack of integration between logistics systems and the AP function created bottlenecks, data discrepancies, and delayed payments to partners. The company needed a managed integration that would create reliable, automated data flow between systems.

What We Did

  • Directed the integration of logistics software with the accounts payable group, mapping data flows and ensuring seamless system connectivity.
  • Documented existing AP and logistics processes in detail, identifying gaps and redesigning workflows to support the integration.
  • Developed a comprehensive data strategy to govern efficient, accurate data exchange between the accounts payable function and the new logistics system.
  • Coordinated with stakeholders across logistics, finance, and IT to ensure integration objectives aligned with broader business goals.

The Results

The integration was delivered within the three-month engagement, creating reliable automated data flow between logistics and AP systems. Process documentation gave the organization a clear foundation for ongoing operations, and the new data strategy reduced manual reconciliation work while improving accuracy in payment processing.
