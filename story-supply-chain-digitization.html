Supply Chain Digitization Roadmap for a Leading Outdoor Apparel Brand

Leading outdoor apparel brand · Consultant · 4 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientLeading outdoor apparel brand
RoleConsultant
Duration4 months
CategorySupply Chain

The Challenge

A leading outdoor apparel brand recognized that its logistics function was operating on manual, fragmented processes that were limiting speed and visibility. Leadership wanted to understand the current state clearly before committing to technology investments — and to identify which inefficiencies were systemic vs. process-driven. They needed a credible assessment and a clear path to digitization.

What We Did

  • Conducted comprehensive logistics workshops to document current-state processes across inbound, outbound, and carrier management functions.
  • Performed root cause analysis to identify the underlying drivers of logistics inefficiencies, producing clear, prioritized findings for leadership.
  • Supported leadership in developing digital transformation recommendations, evaluating advanced technology options including SAP and Project 44.
  • Facilitated initial engagement sessions with software partners to explore fit, capabilities, and implementation approach.

The Results

The engagement produced a clear current-state picture of the logistics function and a prioritized set of recommendations for digital transformation. Leadership had the evidence and analysis needed to make a confident technology investment decision, along with established vendor relationships to accelerate the next phase.
