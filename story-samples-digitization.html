Sample Process Digitization: 58% Reduction, $1.6M Saved

Global footwear brand · Project Manager · 7 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientGlobal footwear brand
RoleProject Manager
Duration7 months
CategoryTechnology

The Challenge

A global footwear brand's sample ordering process was entirely offline — managed through spreadsheets, emails, and phone calls. The result was excessive sample quantities ordered each season, with limited visibility into what was requested, by whom, and why. Leadership estimated significant waste in sample spend but needed rigorous analysis and a systematic solution, not blanket cuts.

What We Did

  • Led a project to reengineer the sample procurement process from an offline, manual workflow to a systematic online process with clear rules and accountability.
  • Applied process mapping and data analytics to identify where excess sample orders originated, developing targeted reduction strategies rather than blanket cuts.
  • Developed new tracking processes, reporting dashboards, and analytics to give leadership real-time visibility into sample spend and ordering patterns.
  • Coordinated with teams across design, development, and merchandising to ensure smooth adoption of the new digital process.

The Results

The digital samples process reduced seasonal sample quantities by 58%, generating $1.6M in annual savings. Tracking and analytics gave the organization ongoing visibility into sample spend for the first time, and the online ordering system eliminated the manual processes that had driven excess ordering.

58% reduction in seasonal sample quantities. $1.6M in annual savings. A process that now runs systematically — with full visibility and accountability.
