Cost Transparency Initiative: 2% Materials Reduction, $800K+ Saved

Global footwear brand · Costing Analyst / Project Manager · 4 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientGlobal footwear brand
RoleCosting Analyst / Project Manager
Duration4 months
CategoryStrategy & Operations

The Challenge

A global footwear brand had a portfolio of legacy products with low margins where costing accuracy was inconsistent across factory partners. Leadership suspected that materials usage and cost reporting varied significantly by factory but lacked the analysis to confirm it — or to know where to intervene. The opportunity was real, but it required careful cross-factory analysis before any changes could be made.

What We Did

  • Led a product cost efficiency project focused specifically on low-margin legacy products, working directly with product engineering and development teams.
  • Evaluated cost transparency and accuracy across multiple factory partners, benchmarking materials usage and identifying meaningful cost variances.
  • Developed and implemented strategies to reduce materials usage by 2% across targeted products without compromising quality or performance.
  • Produced detailed cost analysis and reporting that gave leadership clear visibility into where margin improvement was achievable and how to sustain it.

The Results

The project delivered a 2% reduction in materials usage across targeted products and realized annual savings of over $800K. The analysis work created a foundation of cost transparency the organization had not previously had, and the focused approach — targeting root causes rather than making blanket cuts — preserved product quality while improving margins.

2% reduction in materials usage. Over $800K in annual savings. Achieved without compromising product quality or performance.
