Robotic Cementing Line: From Concept to Production

Major athletic brand · Project Manager · 19 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientMajor athletic brand
RoleProject Manager
Duration19 months
CategoryTechnology

The Challenge

A major athletic brand's manufacturing innovation team wanted to automate a labor-intensive footwear cementing process at their innovation center. The goal was to reduce reliance on hand operators while improving consistency and throughput — but implementing robotics in a precision manufacturing environment required careful specification, testing, and integration work.

What We Did

  • Developed a robotic manufacturing line with Robot Systems Automation, designing the system to replace 20 hand operators with 3 equipment operators on the cementing process.
  • Conducted factory acceptance testing of the robotic equipment in Pisa, Italy, validating readiness before shipment to the innovation center.
  • Ran production pilots at the innovation center to validate the new cementing technologies against finished goods quality standards.
  • Implemented two cementing technologies: traditional adhesive with brushing technology, and PU hotmelt with spray technology, covering multiple product lines.

The Results

The robotic cementing line was successfully deployed at the innovation center, replacing 20 hand operators with just 3 equipment operators while maintaining product quality standards. Factory acceptance testing in Italy ensured the equipment arrived ready for production. The dual-technology implementation gave the brand flexibility to support different product line requirements.

20 hand operators replaced by 3 equipment operators — a step-change in manufacturing efficiency without compromising product quality.
