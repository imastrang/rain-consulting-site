Supply Chain

Domestic Footwear Supply Chain Development and DOD Partnership

Major athletic brand · Project Manager · 19 months

← Back to Success Stories
ClientMajor athletic brand
RoleProject Manager
Duration19 months
CategorySupply Chain

The Challenge

A major athletic brand was looking to develop domestic manufacturing capabilities for footwear — both to support its innovation agenda and to meet the specific requirements of a Department of Defense initiative for Made in USA combat boots. Building a functional North American supply chain required identifying factory partners, transferring advanced manufacturing knowledge, and building the sourcing infrastructure for domestic production.

What We Did

  • Introduced advanced manufacturing technologies to North American factory partners, conducting knowledge transfer from the innovation center to upskill domestic capabilities.
  • Developed and sourced a North American supply chain for footwear production, identifying qualified partners and building the supplier infrastructure.
  • Partnered with the DOD to pilot advanced manufacturing technologies for Made in USA combat boots, meeting specific product specifications and compliance requirements.
  • Conducted in-depth cost comparisons and global production analysis, informing strategic manufacturing decisions across domestic and international regions.

The Results

The North American supply chain was established with capable factory partners producing footwear using technologies transferred from the innovation center. The DOD pilot was completed successfully, demonstrating domestic production viability for combat boots. Cost analysis informed strategic sourcing decisions across the brand's broader manufacturing footprint.
